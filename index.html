Before you land

Weather

About 17°C by day, 8°C at night, with rain on roughly three days in ten. Bring a warm layer and a small umbrella; many restaurants and old buildings are barely heated.

Book ahead

Hubei Provincial Museum is free but needs a real-name slot booked on its official WeChat account, and you enter with the passport you booked with. Closed Mondays. Jianghan Customs House Museum is also free; book on WeChat.

Pay

Link your card to Alipay and WeChat Pay before you fly. Street stalls rarely take anything else, and Alipay's metro QR gets you through the gates.

Apps

Amap (高德地图) for directions, Didi for cars, Trip.com for trains and tickets.

What to eat

Hubei food leans on what the rivers and lakes give: lotus root, freshwater fish, rice, and a lot of sesame paste. Tap a dish name to show it at the counter.

The old names

These shops are behind Wuhan's classic snacks. Most have several branches; search the name on Amap and go to the nearest one.

Crayfish (小龙虾) is Wuhan's summer obsession and runs May to September. In November, fill up on lotus root soup instead.

Getting around

¥2-7

Metro

The fastest way between banks. Line 2 links Hankou with Jiedaokou by Wuhan University; Line 4 stops at the Yellow Crane Tower. Pay with Alipay's metro QR. Runs roughly 6:00 to 23:00.

¥1.5

Yangtze ferry

Commuter boats between Wuhanguan (Hankou) and Zhonghua Road (Wuchang), about every 20 to 30 minutes, 6:30 to 20:00.

¥25-50

Didi

Typical fare across the river between Wuchang and Hankou. After 23:00 it is the only easy way back.

Prices and hours were checked in October 2026 and change often. Confirm on the day, especially for tickets and bookings.
